# Delete your Life Planner account

You can delete your account and all of your data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open Life Planner and go to **You** (top right of Today).
2. Tap your account at the top.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Everything is removed at once, on your phone and on our servers.

## Without the app
Email **support@tribe.az** from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted
Your account, habits, plans, goals, money entries, meals, study, travel and career data, journal entries, mood check-ins, coach chats and settings.

## What we keep
Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous, aggregated usage counts (no names, emails or content) may be kept. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

## Guest accounts
If you only used Life Planner as a guest, your data is stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Questions: support@tribe.az
