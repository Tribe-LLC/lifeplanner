Life Planner: Habits & Goals

## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)
Life Planner is one calm place to plan the parts of life you care about. You choose the areas, and only those show up. Everything stays simple, and everything is connected.

CHOOSE YOUR AREAS
Pick what you want to plan: Habits, Money, Fitness, Meals, Study, Travel, Mind and Career. Skip the rest. You can change your choice at any time.

ADD ANYTHING IN ONE BOX
Type "lunch ramen 12.50" and it lands in Meals and Money. Type a plan, a workout or a date and it goes where it belongs.

A TODAY SCREEN THAT FITS YOUR DAY
See what is due now, later and any time. Check in with a swipe: done, not today, or later. Missed something? Carry it over or let it go, no guilt.

HABITS THAT FORGIVE
Daily, certain days, or a number of times a week. Forgiving streaks, skips with a note, and a gentle review when a habit keeps slipping, so you can keep it, make it easier or let it go.

AREAS BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
- Money: log spending, set budgets, track bills and savings goals.
- Fitness: plan workouts, run a timer, and see your Health Connect activity.
- Meals: plan the week, build a shopping list by aisle, import recipes, track water.
- Study: timers by subject, exams and deadlines with study blocks spread before them.
- Travel: trip pages, budget, packing and a forecast for your days away.
- Mind: a quick mood check-in, a journal, breathing sessions and sleep goals.
- Career: applications, interviews, wins and skills, with next actions.
- Plans: turn a goal such as a 5K or a savings target into dated steps with a pace that adjusts.

A COACH THAT KNOWS YOUR WEEK
Ask for help and the coach answers from your own habits, plans and week, not from a generic script. It can suggest a next step, and you can act on it from the chat. The coach is for motivation and planning. It does not give medical, legal, tax or investment advice.

WORKS WITH YOUR PHONE
- Health Connect: read steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate and weight, and save workouts, meals, water and mindful minutes back.
- Calendar: see your events and add interviews and plans.
- A habits widget for your home screen and gentle reminders you control.

YOUR DATA
Start as a guest, or sign in to keep your data backed up and synced across devices.

Life Planner is a planning tool and not a medical service.
